What the exhibit includes

The original enhancement roadmap has been fully delivered, and a verified form-and-function review was carried out and acted on, batch by batch. The blockers a campus legal, accessibility, or curriculum reviewer checks first are all cleared.

Reactor, physics & visuals

  • Software 3D tokamak with a raymarched WebGL volumetric plasma core that falls back safely to software, supersampled for retina tablets
  • On-screen fusion events, multi-pass bloom, living-plasma turbulence, atmospheric fog
  • Cutaway cross-section, human/scale reference, neutron wall-heat map, visible disruption event, divertor sweep
  • A physically motivated model: triple product, real Lawson curve, energy gain Q, power balance, breeding ratio, burn-through dynamics
  • Depth for the curious: a bremsstrahlung radiated-loss term and a Greenwald density limit, computed live and shown with tooltips and model-card entries
  • A green, net-positive cold-open, enlarged hero numbers, and legible LED annunciator lamps

Teaching & UX

  • A first-visit welcome that offers the 30-second tour or jumping straight in
  • Guided story (spotlighting the control each step names), guided tour, seven missions with hints and a status-strip challenges chip
  • Predict-then-test that keeps a running score and carries it to the takeaway card
  • A state-aware contextual coach, real-world units, model-assumptions card, glossary, Balance-of-Plant flow
  • CSV export, QR takeaway card, student worksheet and instructor key (with grade-band, time, and NGSS metadata), and a booth facilitator guide

Accessibility, robustness & packaging

  • Keyboard operable end to end: the 3D reactor orbits with the arrow keys (plus and minus zoom, R resets); every dialog (Welcome, Tour, Story, Takeaway card, About) traps focus, closes on Escape, and restores focus on close, behind a global focus ring
  • Screen-reader support: sliders announce values with units; the reactor state is announced on change through one polite region
  • Four themes, an end-to-end reduced-motion path, and a vertical-swipe that scrolls the page on mobile while a horizontal swipe orbits
  • All-day kiosk: Present mode, idle watchdog, tab-hide pause, lite-GPU path, WebGL context-loss recovery
  • Adoptable: an MIT license, a one-block BRAND config that puts a host program's name on the topbar, About, and takeaway card, an APP_VERSION with a CHANGELOG.md, and consistent, em-dash-free copy

Deliberately left out

Recorded so the choices are clear to whoever maintains the project next. Each was considered and declined on purpose.

Out of scope

The larger "v2" bets (a Three.js render track, a two-player race to ignition, and a WebXR booth mode) were scrapped: each would add a dependency and ongoing maintenance for little outreach gain, and they cut against the zero-dependency, single-folder design that makes this exhibit easy to donate and run. The one physics idea not pursued, a felt reactivity rollover, was also left out: making it visible would mean raising the plasma temperature ceiling and re-tuning every preset, a poor trade against the bremsstrahlung and density-limit depth that already ships.

A pre-donation QA pass

Worth running once on the actual event hardware before handing it over. It is the difference between "works on my machine" and "works at the booth."

  • Open index.html straight from the folder (file://) with no server, in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.
  • Run on a real mid-range tablet for at least an hour; watch for heat, throttling, and frame-rate decay.
  • Open it on a phone, scan-to-run style, and confirm you can scroll the whole page past the reactor.
  • Project it in a bright room and read the hero numbers and lamps from across the room.
  • Drive each preset and each mission to completion; confirm the state labels, lamps, and coach text agree, and re-check the instructor key against the live values.
  • Do a keyboard-only pass and a screen-reader pass; orbit the reactor and open every dialog with the keyboard.
  • Toggle the operating-system reduced-motion setting and confirm the stilled experience is still coherent.
  • On a fresh browser, confirm the welcome prompt appears once and both buttons dismiss it.
  • Leave it idle for several minutes and confirm it returns to a friendly state.
  • Set the BRAND block to the host program and confirm the topbar, About, and takeaway card update.
  • Re-verify the real-machine figures (ITER, SPARC, EAST move) at donation time.

Three things to protect

Portability: keep it openable from one folder with no network. Accessibility: keep the reduced-motion, contrast, colorblind, keyboard, and screen-reader paths first-class as you add features. Performance: gate every new heavy addition behind the same idle, visibility, and lite checks, so it stays smooth on the weakest machine in the room.
